# Acceptable Use Policy

I built SideWave because I needed a dictation tool that actually understands technical vocabulary, keeps focus music playing, and handles punctuation correctly—without relying on cloud APIs. SideWave is designed to be a clean, private utility that transcribes your speech and drops the text directly into whatever app you aim it at.

To do this securely, SideWave runs entirely on-device. It uses NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT model directly on the Mac's Neural Engine via CoreML for offline speech recognition, alongside local language models (Gemma 4 and Apple Intelligence) to refine and sanitize the text. No audio ever leaves your machine.

**Because I purposely never built any telemetry into SideWave, I have no automated way of knowing how people use the app or when things break. The only way I can improve this tool is if you manually provide feedback.**

Because SideWave operates 100% locally, I cannot monitor, see, or control what you choose to dictate. **Therefore, you assume all responsibility and liability for your inputs and how you use this software.**

By using SideWave, you agree to the following rules:

* **Do not use this tool for malicious purposes.** This includes attempting to dictate, process, generate, or refine hate speech, illegal content, harassment, or sexually explicit material.
* **I am not liable for your actions.** SideWave includes optional profanity filtering, and the Refine feature can help catch and clean up inappropriate content before it lands in your target app. However, these are assistive tools, not guarantees. You are ultimately responsible for the text you dictate and drop into your target windows.
* **Respect third-party boundaries.** Since SideWave sends text directly into other applications (like external LLM chat windows or web browsers), you are responsible for ensuring that what you dictate complies with the terms of service of those receiving apps.

Please use common sense and do not use my code to do stupid or illegal things.

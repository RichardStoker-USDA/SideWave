# Acceptable Use Policy

I built SideWave because I needed a dictation tool that understands technical vocabulary, keeps focus music playing, and handles punctuation correctly, without relying on cloud APIs. SideWave transcribes your speech on your Mac and pastes the text into the app you're working in.

It recognizes speech with NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT model on the Mac's Neural Engine through Core ML, and refines the text with a local language model (Gemma 4 or Apple Intelligence). Your audio and text are never uploaded.

**I didn't build any telemetry into SideWave, so I have no way of knowing how people use it or when it breaks. If something goes wrong, please tell me in a GitHub issue.**

Because SideWave runs locally, I can't see or control what you dictate. **Therefore, you assume all responsibility and liability for your inputs and how you use this software.**

By using SideWave, you agree to the following rules:

* **Do not use this tool for malicious purposes.** This includes attempting to dictate, process, generate, or refine hate speech, illegal content, harassment, or sexually explicit material.
* **I am not liable for your actions.** SideWave has an optional profanity filter, but it's a convenience, not a guarantee. You are responsible for the text you dictate and send to other apps.
* **Follow other apps' terms.** SideWave pastes into other apps, such as browsers and AI chat windows. What you send them has to follow their terms of service.

Please use common sense and do not use my code to do stupid or illegal things.

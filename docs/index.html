SideWave

Dictation that works the way you work.

Focus any text field, press your hotkey, and speak. A live preview floats on screen so you see every word as it lands. Move between windows, keep music in your headphones, teach it your jargon.

Download for Mac View source

Version 0.4.0 macOS 26 or later Apple silicon Free and open source

Wherever you were typing

Slack, Mail, a terminal, a note. SideWave remembers the app and the text field you started in, so you can look something up in another window while you talk. It checks the target again before it pastes.

If an app can't confirm the paste, the text waits in History instead of landing twice. Press Escape to stop without sending, and the text stays on Home until you want it.

Templates

Standard fixes grammar and filler words and keeps your voice. Professional and Concise change the tone.

Email, Instructions, AI Prompt, and Custom write a document instead. Read it, edit it, or say what to change, like "make the second paragraph shorter."

Pick an output language to dictate in one language and get the text in another.

Teach it your jargon

Add the words a general speech model gets wrong: species, assays, tools, people. List what each one gets misheard as, and add a note so Refine knows what it means.

Glossaries live on your Mac. Optional bundles add a curated word list for a field, and they only update when you ask.

The app

Most of the time SideWave is a menu bar icon and a hotkey. Open it to change the template, model, or microphone, look back through History, or manage your glossaries.

Readiness, the hotkey, and the choices you change most: template, Refine model, microphone, and where the text goes.

It stays on your Mac

Speech recognition runs on the Neural Engine with NVIDIA Parakeet, and Refine uses Apple Intelligence or Gemma 4 on your Mac. Dictation audio stays in memory and is never saved. There's no account and no telemetry.

SideWave goes online for three things: downloading models, fetching glossary bundles you ask for, and checking for updates if you allow it. Data Transparency in the Help menu lists every request. The privacy policy has the details.

Get SideWave

Free and open source under Apache 2.0, for macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac. Open the DMG, drag SideWave to Applications, and follow setup. It explains each permission before macOS asks, then downloads the speech models.

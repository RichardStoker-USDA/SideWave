SideWave

Dictation that runs on your Mac and types where you're working.

Tap Right Option and talk. SideWave transcribes your speech on the Mac, cleans it up with a local language model, and pastes it into the app you were using. There's no account or subscription, and what you say stays on your Mac.

Download for Mac Source on GitHub

Version 0.3.0 macOS 26 or later Apple silicon Free and open source

What you see while you talk

A small preview sits at the bottom of the screen while you dictate. It shows each stage of the text, so you can tell what's settled before anything is pasted.

Draft
Gray words come from a streaming speech model and appear a moment after you say them. They can still change.
Confirmed
NVIDIA Parakeet rechecks each phrase. Words it confirms turn solid; words it corrects change in place.
Refined
Apple Intelligence or Gemma fixes punctuation, filler words, and misheard terms. When you stop, the result goes to the app you started in.

If Refine fails or runs out of time, you get the recognized text instead of nothing. Press Escape to stop without sending; the text waits on Home.

Pick how it writes

Standard keeps your voice and fixes the mechanics. Professional smooths the tone, and Concise shortens it. These paste straight into your app.

Email, Instructions, AI Prompt, and Custom open a document instead. Read it, edit it, or say what to change ("make the second paragraph shorter") and Refine applies it.

Set an output language to dictate in one language and get the text in another. Check names and numbers in anything you translate.

The app

Most of the time SideWave is a menu bar icon and a shortcut. When you open it, this is what's there.

Readiness, the shortcut, and the choices you change most: template, Refine model, microphone, and where the text goes.

Details

Speech recognition
NVIDIA Parakeet TDT v3 (multilingual) on the Neural Engine, with a streaming model for the live draft. Parakeet Ultra is optional.
Refine
Apple Intelligence where your Mac supports it, or Gemma 4 E2B, E4B, or 12B through MLX (about 3, 5.2, and 6.8 GB). 12B suits Macs with 24 GB of memory or more.
Vocabulary
Your own glossaries, plus optional bundles from a public catalog. Replacements and Refine hints, per term.
Shortcut
Right Option by default. Tap to start and stop, hold for push-to-talk, Escape to stop without sending.
Delivery
Pastes into the app that was focused when you started, or a window you choose. If a paste can't be confirmed, the text stays on Home and in History.
Meetings
Optional. Records locally, labels up to eight speakers, and writes notes with a local model.
Requirements
macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
License
Free. Apache 2.0, with model licenses listed in the acknowledgments.

What leaves your Mac

Your speech and your text don't. Recognition, Refine, and meeting notes run locally, and dictation audio is processed in memory without being saved. SideWave has no accounts, analytics, or crash reporting.

It does go online for three things:

  • Downloading speech and Refine models from Hugging Face.
  • Downloading glossary bundles you ask for, from GitHub.
  • Checking for updates, if you allow it when SideWave asks.

Help > Data Transparency shows each of those requests. Text you paste into another app follows that app's rules. The privacy policy has the details.

Install

  1. Download SideWave.dmg, open it, and drag SideWave to Applications.
  2. Open SideWave. Setup explains Microphone and Accessibility access before macOS asks for them.
  3. Let the speech models download. Setup shows the size and progress.
  4. Click into any text field, tap Right Option, and talk.

Updates install from SideWave > Check for Updates. To build it yourself, see the README.

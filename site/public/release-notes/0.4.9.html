  • Keep dictation audio, in Settings > Privacy in Advanced view and off by default. It saves the microphone audio of each dictation on this Mac so missing or extra words can be checked against what you said. SideWave keeps the newest 100 recordings, up to 2 GB, in a folder only your account can open, with Show in Finder and Delete All. Nothing is uploaded.
